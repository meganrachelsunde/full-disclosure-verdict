# Project Scope — Full Disclosure Verdict

## What this product is

Full Disclosure Verdict is a **pre-expert dental-record intelligence and
screening application**. It helps legal teams organize complex dental
record productions into:

- A source-traceable **record inventory**
- A **source-linked chronology** of treatment events
- A **missing-document and gap detection** log
- A **contradiction preservation** log
- Structured materials for **attorney and licensed-expert handoff**

The purpose is organizational and screening work that happens *before*
a licensed dental expert conducts their own independent analysis.

## What this product is not

Full Disclosure Verdict does **not** provide:

- Legal advice
- Diagnoses
- Standard-of-care opinions
- Causation opinions
- Damages calculations
- Case-merit conclusions

Any output that looks like inventory, chronology, gap, or contradiction
data must remain a plain observation tied to a source document — never
an interpretation, opinion, or conclusion. Evidence, inference, and
professional conclusions are kept in clearly separate categories at all
times.

## Current build status

This is an **initial build**. It contains:

- A marketing/informational landing page
- A clearly marked fictional demonstration area (no real patient data)
- Governance and scope documentation

It does **not** yet contain:

- Document upload functionality
- Any handling of real patient or case records
- Authentication, storage, or data-processing pipelines

Document upload and real-record handling will not be built until a
security and privacy architecture has been reviewed and explicitly
approved.

## Audience

- Attorneys and legal teams preparing dental-record productions for
  review
- Licensed dental experts retained to provide professional opinions

This tool organizes the file. It does not replace the professional
judgment of either party.
